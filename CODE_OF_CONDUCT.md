# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

**In short:** be respectful, assume good faith, focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated anywhere in
this project's spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, or elsewhere.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/raccioly/testguard/security/advisories/new)
or email the maintainer listed in `package.json`. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
